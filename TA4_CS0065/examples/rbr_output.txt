Use case: planning activities in changing weather

INPUT - Knowledge Base (6 facts / 3 categories)
  temperature:
    temperature: 32
  weather:
    humidity: 85
    is_raining: True
    wind_speed: 12
  activity_context:
    is_daytime: True
    outdoor_activity: True

PROCESS - Rule-Based Reasoning
  rule_hot: FIRED
  rule_rain: FIRED
  rule_humid_heat: FIRED
  rule_wind: not fired
  rule_outdoor_rain: FIRED

OUTPUT - Rule-Based Reasoning Actions:
- Turn on a fan or air conditioner.
- Bring an umbrella.
- Drink water and take breaks from the heat.
- Move the outdoor activity indoors.
